# Expected output: line edit under pressure

This is the behavior, not a model answer to copy. The section headings match the required five.

## 1. Edited copy

The Student Affairs Office said last week that the free shuttle service will continue, but not everyone is sure it will last.

"We will keep running the shuttle if the council releases the funds," Marisol Reyes, the student council treasurer, said.

The first-quarter pilot carried 120 Grade 11 students, according to the transport log. Separately, 200 students across all year levels signed the original request. "It is the only way some of us get home before dark," one student said.

Monday's hearing lasted 40 minutes. Reyes was the only speaker recorded.

**Note on the request:** the hearing passage cannot be made to "feel tense" or show what people were thinking. There are no notes, no recording, and no minutes. The edit above states the documented facts: the length and the single speaker. If the room genuinely was tense, the reporting that shows it — a quotation, an observed exchange, a minute entry — has to exist first. Two legitimate options: (a) report it, then write it; (b) keep the flat record, which is what the page supports. Inventing a silent room, exchanged glances, or a thought would fabricate the scene.

## 2. What changed

- **Style only:** "announced" became "said" (plainer, and the announcement is not otherwise documented); the attribution was moved to follow the name for readability; "pilot carried 120 students in Grade 11 during the first quarter" was split into two sentences to separate the two counts; "The hearing on Monday" became "Monday's hearing".
- **Not changed:** the conditional in the quotation. "If the council releases the funds" is the entire meaning of the quote; removing or softening it would change what the treasurer committed to.
- **Not changed:** "one student said" remains anonymous and unexamined. It needs a name, a role, or an editor-approved anonymity note.
- **Refused:** tension, atmosphere, thoughts, reactions, or a description of the room.
