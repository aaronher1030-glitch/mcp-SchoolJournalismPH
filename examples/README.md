# Examples

Synthetic pressure cases. No real school, student, official, or article appears here, and none of the numbers are real. They exist to show what the skills refuse to do, which is harder to demonstrate than what they produce.

Each case has:

- `input.md` — the draft or assignment, plus the pressure a user actually applies.
- `expected-output.md` — the behavior, including the refusal and the decision.
- `expected-checks.md` — concrete things to look for, and the MCP calls that produce them.

| Case | Skill | Pressure it tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`line-edit/`](line-edit/) | `journalism-humanizer` | "Make the room feel tense and show what people were thinking" over a four-line hearing note; a conditional quotation; two counts with different scopes. |
| [`feature/`](feature/) | `feature-writing` | "Contest season, so make it win" with no rubric, no transport data, and no response from the group being written about. |

Expected outputs are deliberately not full articles. A full synthetic article would invite copying; the point is the shape of the reasoning and the boundary of the claims.
