# Feature assignment: dismissal change

Synthetic case. Fictional school, fictional people, invented details. Not reporting.

## The request

> Write a feature for our school paper about the new 4:30 p.m. dismissal. Contest season, so make it win. 800 words, English.

## Supplied reporting

**Interview — Principal Danilo Cruz, 12 minutes, recorded (00:02:10–00:14:30).**
Said the last class now ends at 4:10 and dismissal moved to 4:30 "because the service road is at its worst between 4:10 and 4:40." No data was offered on how many students use public transport. Did not answer a follow-up about whether the change was reviewed with the transport group.

**Interview — two Grade 10 students, 6 minutes, recorded.**
Student A: waits "about 45 minutes" for a jeepney after dismissal. Student B: walks home, "around 20 minutes." Neither was asked how they traveled before the change.

**Document — School Memo No. 12, s. 2026.**
New dismissal time effective November 3. One page. Does not state a reason.

**Not supplied:** the contest rubric, the number of students who take public transport, any response from the transport or jeepney group, any parent comment, any record of how the 4:30 time was chosen.

## What the writer wants

An 800-word contest feature that opens with a student waiting at the gate.
